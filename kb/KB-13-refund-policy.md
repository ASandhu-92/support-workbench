# KB-13 Refund policy

- **Duplicate charges** are always refunded in full, for the duplicate only.
- **Subscription charges** (Pro or Team) can be refunded if you ask within 7 days of the charge and
  used fewer than 50 credits since that charge. The plan is then cancelled, and the account moves to
  Free.
- **Credit packs** can be refunded within 14 days if none of the pack's credits were used.
- Outside these rules we do not normally refund, but a support lead can make an exception.

How refunds work:
- Every refund is approved by a person on the support team; the AI assistant cannot issue one.
- Refunds go back to the original card. They show on your statement in 5 to 10 business days,
  depending on your bank.
- Once a refund is issued we send a confirmation email with the refund amount and date.

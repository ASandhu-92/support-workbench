# KB-09 Team workspaces, seats and ownership

A Team workspace (KB-03) includes 5 seats. Extra seats are $10 per month each and are added to the
next invoice, prorated for the rest of the month.

- **Invite:** Settings > Members > Invite, by email. The invite link is valid for 7 days.
- **Roles:** Owner (billing and deleting the workspace), Admin (members and settings), Member
  (build and deploy).
- **Transfer ownership:** the current Owner goes to Settings > Members, picks an Admin, and chooses
  "Make owner". Only the Owner can do this. If the Owner has left the company and cannot log in,
  support can transfer ownership after verifying the request with a second Admin of the workspace
  and the billing email on file.
- Removing a member frees their seat right away. Their projects stay in the workspace.

# KB-02 How credits work

Every AI generation (a prompt that changes your app) uses credits. Simple edits use 1 credit;
large multi-file changes use up to 5. The exact cost is shown before you send a prompt.

| Plan | Monthly credits |
|---|---|
| Free | 50 |
| Pro | 500 |
| Team | 2,000, shared by the whole workspace |

- Monthly credits reset on your billing date, not on the 1st of the month. Free accounts reset on
  the date the account was created.
- Unused monthly credits do **not** roll over.
- Credit packs (200 credits for $10) can be bought on any paid plan. Pack credits never expire and
  are used only after your monthly credits run out.
- A generation that fails with an error is not supposed to use credits. If you were charged credits
  for a failed generation, contact support with the project name and time; that is a bug.
- You can see your usage under Settings > Usage, per day and per project.

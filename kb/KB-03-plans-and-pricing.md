| | Free | Pro | Team |

- Prices are in US dollars and do not include sales tax or VAT where it applies.
- Plans are billed monthly. There is no annual plan at the moment.
- A credit pack is a one-time $10 purchase for 200 extra credits (see KB-02).
- You can change plan at any time; see KB-15 for how upgrades and downgrades are charged.

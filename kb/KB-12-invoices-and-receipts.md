# KB-12 Invoices and receipts

Every payment has an invoice or receipt. To get them: Settings > Billing > **Manage billing**. This
opens the billing portal, where you can download every past invoice as a PDF.

- **Company name, address or VAT/tax ID:** add them in the billing portal under "Billing
  information". They appear on all **future** invoices.
- **Past invoices** cannot be edited once issued, because they are tax documents. If you need the
  company details on an invoice that was already issued, contact support; we can issue a copy with
  your details added for invoices from the last 90 days.
- Receipts are also emailed to the billing email on the account when the payment succeeds.

# KB-10 Account, email and login

- **Change your email:** Settings > Account > Email. We send a confirmation link to the new
  address; the change happens when you click it. The billing email on your subscription changes at
  the same time.
- **Can't log in:** use "Forgot password". If you signed up with Google or GitHub, log in with the
  same button; there is no separate password.
- **Two-factor authentication:** Settings > Account > Security. If you lose your 2FA device, use a
  backup code. Without a backup code, support must verify your identity before resetting 2FA, which
  takes 1 to 2 business days.
- **Delete your account:** Settings > Account > Delete. Paid plans must be cancelled first. Deleted
  accounts and their projects are removed after 30 days (KB-11).

**Customer impact.** Amy Walsh says every generation is charged twice the credits shown before sending, so her credits run out twice as fast. Because it started with a platform update, it may affect all paid and free accounts. Wrong charges across many accounts is a sev1 until engineering rules it out.

**Evidence (quoted from the ticket)**
- Since the update on Monday, every generation takes double the credits shown before I send it.
- The prompt says 2 credits and my usage page shows 4 used.
- Happened on every prompt today.
- Screenshot of Settings > Usage attached.

**Already tried by the customer**
- Compared the credit cost shown before sending a prompt with the amount on Settings > Usage, and attached a screenshot. She does not mention any other steps.

**KB checked:** KB-02, KB-03. KB-02 says the cost shown before sending is what is charged and only covers refunds for failed generations, so a consistent 2x overcharge on successful generations is a billing or metering bug that needs an engineer.

**Questions for engineering**
- Did Monday's release change how generation credits are deducted or recorded, for example a duplicate usage event per generation?
- Are other accounts showing deducted credits at 2x the pre-send estimate since Monday, and how many?
- Once the cause is fixed, can affected accounts have their overcharged credits restored, and how do we identify Amy's?

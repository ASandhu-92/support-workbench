**KB checked:** KB-04, KB-05, KB-08, KB-11. KB-11 says support restores the daily database snapshots (kept 7 days), but it does not explain why a text-only redeploy emptied the live database, and a possible platform-wide cause needs engineering before any restore.

**Questions for engineering**
